Tester terms

Last updated 7 November 2026

Thanks for helping me test Castaway Island. These are the ground rules, in plain words. By signing up you agree to them.

1. Who I am

Castaway Island is made by So Sun Games. When this page says “I” or “me”, that’s who it means. You can reach me at soe.sun.games@gmail.com, or with the DM dev button in the game.

2. The game is mine

Castaway Island, its idea, its name, its characters, its story, its art, its code and everything else in it belong to me. Being a tester gives you a personal right to install and play the test version while the test runs. It doesn’t give you any ownership of the game or the right to copy, sell, rebuild or redistribute it, or any part of it.

3. Your feedback

Anything you send me about the game (bug reports, ideas, screenshots, your opinions) I’m free to use to make the game better, without owing you anything for it, now or later. You keep the right to say whatever you like about the game elsewhere.

4. Talking about it

You’re welcome to talk about the game and share screenshots or short clips. Please don’t share the test build itself, your invite link, or files from inside the game.

5. It’s a test

This is an unfinished game. It might crash, lose your progress, drain your battery or just act weird. It’s given to you as it is, with no promises. To the extent the law allows, I’m not responsible for any loss or damage that comes from playing it. Things in the test may change or disappear before the game is released, and the test can end at any time.

6. Your email and what the game sends

  • I keep the email, phone type and anything you choose to write on the sign up form. I use it only to invite you to the test and to send you things about this test.
  • I never sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a newsletter.
  • It’s stored with Supabase (servers in the EU). Your email also goes to Apple (TestFlight) or Google (Google Play) so they can send your invite.
  • When you send a DM dev report, it includes what you wrote, any picture you attach, and a short log of your play session with basic device info, so I can find the problem.
  • The game can send anonymous play statistics to help me balance it. You can turn that off in Settings under Share play data.
  • Want your email removed? Write to me and I’ll delete it. I’ll also delete the sign up list once testing is over.

7. Leaving

You can stop testing whenever you like. Just delete the app, and tell me if you want your email removed. I can also remove testers from the test, for example if a build gets shared around.

8. Changes

If these terms change, I’ll update the date at the top. If it’s a big change, I’ll let testers know.
